## 1. Approval boundary

- The matrix uses the recommendations in Section 18 of `docs/PHASE_2_DOMAIN_DECISIONS.md` as its starting point.
- The owner may confirm, amend, or reject each proposal. A proposal remains open until the responsible product/school, technical, and privacy/security owners record an explicit decision.
- The existing migration gate remains closed for any schema area whose O2 dependency is unresolved. Existing migrations remain immutable.
- The Indonesian identity conventions in O2-01 are proposed conventions, not silent assumptions that every Guru or Siswa has every official identifier.
- Exact school-specific values—grade list, term count, required documents, document formats and limits, MFA factor/recovery, workload/SLOs, retention periods, RPO/RTO, and restore ownership—must be supplied or confirmed by the owner; this draft does not invent them.

## 2. Inherited non-negotiable boundaries

The proposals below remain subject to the preserved project and Phase 0/1 decisions:

- **Scope and access:** single school only; Indonesian school context; roles remain `administrator`, `guru`, and `siswa`; authorization uses native Laravel middleware, Form Requests, Policies/Gates, ownership checks, and query scoping. A custom permission system is deferred unless a documented requirement proves native mechanisms insufficient.
- **People privacy:** no public student directory; no public identifiable student achievement or recipient data; NIK, NISN, student numbers, and other sensitive identifiers are never public. Guru access is assignment-scoped, and historical assignments do not grant current student access.
- **Academic and history:** academic years, ordered terms/semesters, numeric grade levels, one active class placement per student per academic year, and retained historical enrollment rows. No program/major entity is added without a real school requirement; academic and audit history is not hard-deleted by ordinary workflows.
- **PPDB:** applicants never automatically become internal users; selection is administrator-controlled and auditable; there is no automated ranking in the first release; opaque access, soft duplicate detection, explicit consent/version records, private documents, controlled conversion, and a separate post-commit student-account invitation are required.
- **Public content:** publication is administrator-controlled; Guru contribution is optional and only through an explicitly authorized, ownership-scoped ability. Public Guru projections use a minimum approved field allowlist. Publicly published media requires moderation and applicable consent; public images also require metadata sanitation, and private documents are never publicly exposed.
- **Data and time:** retain academic/audit history by default; use lifecycle/archive/hold-aware processes instead of routine hard deletion; store timestamps in UTC and present them in `Asia/Jakarta`.
- **Infrastructure and performance:** MariaDB remains the source of truth; Redis is infrastructure for appropriate cache, production session, rate-limit, and queue workloads, not business truth. Approximately 1,000 concurrent users is a planning target, not a guaranteed capacity claim; capacity and SLO claims require measurable tests.
- **Operations and security:** administrator MFA is mandatory for production; audit logging, rate limiting, secure file validation, private storage, encrypted backups, tested database/private-media restore, documented RPO/RTO, and `Asia/Jakarta` presentation are required. Secrets remain outside the repository.

## 4. Decision-use notes

- Approval of one O2 item does not automatically approve every dependent item or migration area; the dependency column must also be satisfied.
- School-specific identifiers, academic calendars, document requirements, MFA recovery, retention periods, performance targets, RPO/RTO, and operational owners are inputs the owner must confirm rather than values supplied by this draft.
- Any request to expose student data, add an internal applicant/guardian role, introduce multi-school tenancy, or add a custom permission system requires a new explicit decision outside this draft.
